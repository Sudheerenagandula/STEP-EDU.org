Why Hyderabad?

As the capital of Telangana, Hyderabad is a magnet for talent from across the state and country. With over 100+ PG colleges and thousands of unemployed graduates each year, the need for a structured finishing school is immense.

Even capturing just 1% of graduating students — roughly 2,000 students — at a minimum fee of ₹30,000 for the 6-month program generates ₹6 crore per annum per center.

With multiple centers, this number multiplies. And STEP is first of its kind in India focused on professional undergraduates — making the opportunity even larger.
